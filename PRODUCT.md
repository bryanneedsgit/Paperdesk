# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Paperdesk is for macOS and Windows users who need to work with PDFs locally: opening, reading, combining, rearranging, deleting, rotating, formatting, annotating, filling forms, and exporting documents without uploading files online.

## Product Purpose

Paperdesk is an offline-first PDF reader, editor, formatter, and form utility. Success means users can quickly complete common PDF workflows in one desktop workspace with clear page selection, predictable tools, and confidence that files remain local and private.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, functional. The interface should feel like a serious desktop productivity tool, closer to Preview, Finder, Linear, and professional document software than to a marketing website.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated SaaS styling, excessive gradients, unnecessary cards, playful illustrations, over-rounded blobs, decorative surfaces, and heavy marketing-page composition. The app should not feel like a landing page.

## Design Principles

Functional first: the active document, selected pages, and available actions should always be easy to understand.

Local and private: visual language should reinforce an offline desktop-tool feel, not a cloud service or upload workflow.

Compact but not cramped: keep controls efficient for repeated work while preserving enough rhythm for scanning.

Clear workspace hierarchy: toolbars, page thumbnails, document canvas, and inspector panels should have distinct roles.

Strong state clarity: hover, focus, disabled, selected, drag, and drop states should be visible and consistent.

## UI Structure

Top toolbar for primary actions.

Left thumbnail sidebar for page management.

Center PDF viewer canvas.

Right inspector panel for document tools, forms, formatting, annotations, and export.

Bottom status bar for page count, zoom, source file, and unsaved state.

## Visual Direction

Desktop productivity app.

Inspired by Preview, Finder, Linear, and professional document tools.

Neutral background with a navy or slate accent.

Clear focus, hover, disabled, selected, and drag states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use accessible contrast for text and controls, preserve keyboard-visible focus, avoid relying on color alone for critical states, and keep motion minimal and state-driven.
